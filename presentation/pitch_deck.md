# MedSynapse AI — Hackathon Pitch Deck & Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Product:** MedSynapse AI
- **Tagline:** *Reconstruct the patient's journey. Surface what matters.*
- **Core Statement:** "We don't just summarize medical records—we reconstruct the patient's clinical journey and surface documented gaps that doctors need to review."
- **Official Brand:** MedSynapse canonical wordmark with integrated ECG pulse and neural synapse.

---

## Slide 2: The Healthcare Crisis: Disjointed Records
- 150+ pages per chronic patient spread across disjointed PDFs, prescriptions, and lab portals.
- 40% of clinical time spent hunting for prior medication changes and investigation results.
- Dangerous adverse drug events caused by buried documentation contradictions.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution: A Clinical Intelligence Operating System
- Ingests multimodal records (scanned PDFs, handwritten notes, tables).
- Reconstructs a longitudinal journey with multi-relational graph edges.
- Synchronized 3-Panel Synapse View: Timeline ⟷ Clinical Intelligence ⟷ Evidence Rail.

---

## Slide 4: Unique "WOW" Innovations
1. **Reconstruct Patient Journey (1-Click Pipeline):** Multi-stage synthesis surfacing What Happened, What Changed, What Is Missing, What Conflicts.
2. **Clinical Gap Radar:** Proactively surfaces missing diagnostic reports and unscheduled follow-ups.
3. **Change Lens & Medication Evolution:** Traces drug dose changes with documented reasons.
4. **Second Look:** Automated evidence audit ensuring 100% source backing before handoffs.

---

## Slide 5: Genuine IBM Technology Integration
- **IBM watsonx.ai:** Granite foundation models for structured medical JSON reasoning and zero hallucination.
- **IBM watsonx Discovery:** Exact page-and-character-level coordinate retrieval.
- **IBM Bob Integration:** Clinical assist event hooks for background asynchronous alerts.

---

## Slide 6: Market Impact & Clinical Workflow
- Reduces handoff preparation from 35 minutes to under 2 minutes.
- Catches dosage contradictions before pharmacy dispensing.
- ABDM / ABHA ready and FHIR R4 interoperable.
